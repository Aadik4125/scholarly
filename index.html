Scholarly: check your research paper before you submit

Scholarly is a free AI research and academic integrity copilot. Upload your paper or essay as a PDF or Word file and get a full review in a couple of minutes.

  • Consistency checks: catch contradictions in numbers, datasets, methods and models between sections.
  • Writing suggestions: clearer, more academic wording that keeps your meaning.
  • Citation gaps: find strong claims that need a source.
  • Similarity check: compare your text with published research from OpenAlex and CORE.
  • AI-content detection: estimate how much of the text reads as AI-generated.
  • Ask My Paper: ask questions about your own paper, like a practice viva.

Your paper stays in your browser. Our server analyzes it and keeps no copy.
